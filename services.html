Our Tailored Care Services

Home / Services

Accompanied Activities

We support clients to participate in activities they enjoy and need to attend, providing companionship and assistance to help them remain active, engaged, and connected with their community.

Assistance with Self-Care and Activities of Daily Living

Our care team provides respectful support with everyday personal tasks such as bathing, dressing, grooming, mobility, eating, and other daily activities to help maintain independence and wellbeing.

Assistance with the Self-Administration of Medication

We assist clients to safely manage their prescribed medications by providing reminders, preparation support, and supervision in accordance with their care plan and healthcare provider instructions.

Continence Management (Non-Clinical)

We provide practical support with continence-related needs, helping clients maintain comfort, dignity, and confidence through assistance with personal routines and continence products.

Cultural Support

We deliver culturally sensitive care that respects each person's language, traditions, beliefs, and preferences, helping clients stay connected to their cultural identity and community.

Direct Transport

We provide transportation services to help clients travel safely to appointments, social activities, shopping trips, and other important destinations.

Flexible Respite

Our respite services offer temporary support for clients while providing family members and carers with time to rest, attend personal commitments, or take a well-deserved break.

General House Cleaning

We help maintain a clean, safe, and comfortable home environment through routine household cleaning tasks tailored to each client's needs.

Group Social Support

We encourage social connection and community participation through group activities, outings, and programs designed to promote wellbeing, friendship, and engagement.

Indirect Transport

We assist clients to access transport services and make travel arrangements, helping them maintain access to appointments, community activities, and essential services.

Individual Social Support

Our one-on-one social support services provide companionship and meaningful interaction, helping reduce social isolation and support emotional wellbeing.

Laundry Services

We assist with washing, drying, folding, ironing, and other laundry-related tasks to help clients maintain a comfortable and well-organised home.

Shopping Assistance

We help clients with grocery shopping and other essential purchases, either by accompanying them to shops or assisting with shopping on their behalf.

Nursing Assistant Clinical Care (Direct)

Our qualified nursing assistants deliver critical, hands-on professional clinical care and physical health monitoring in alignment with certified care plans.

Nursing Assistant Clinical Care (Indirect)

Our nursing assistants provide essential behind-the-scenes support including documenting care activities, communicating with healthcare professionals and family, preparing care environments, and coordinating ongoing care.

Need a Tailored Care Package or Custom Routine?

Our professional care team is ready to formulate a care plan that perfectly respects your choices and lifestyle.
